Section IV
CUSTOMER SCOPE OF WORK
The tender excludes, the following items and it shall be responsibility of customers to provide these to meet our requirements.
CUSTOMER SCOPE OF WORK
1 Storing of material at site 30-55 Sq. Meters (space at ground floor), with dry, Ventilated and illuminated storage space protected from theft and damage, weather protection and locking arrangement within 10-meter radius before material delivery at site. This room will remain in possession of installation engineer till erection is completed and handed over.
2 Construction of lift shaft and overhead as per general Arrangement drawing, Builders work such as forming Lift Well, pit and overhead as per IS norms 9” brick or 6” RCC which shall be dry and free from dust with necessary access according to local regulations and all minor builders work such as cutting away etc., and also Lift Shaft painting/White wash with 2 coats.
NOTE: For a Machine Room Type Elevator, the Machine Room shall have proper access (Stair case – Steel / Brick) from the roof/Top floor.
Load hooks or I beam for lifting the machine designed for 2000 kg load.
3 Dividing beams for multiple wells and inter-well pit screens shall be provided if required. Pit should be waterproofed.
4 Entrance shall be rough finished as per drawings and Guarding on the openings on all floor.
5 Lift Scaffolding, Planks and ladders to fix in the shaft as per our requirements by the client.
6 Fees for inspection of the Lift by the lift Inspector or Electrical inspector or any other fees Charge by the surveyor or Corporation or Municipality or other Government agencies to be borne by client.
ELECTRICALS
1 Working lights for shaft and temporary and permanent electricity supplies. Cost of electricity and water to be provided by the client to carry out the erection and testing work.
2 Wiring of main and earth wires from the source of supply to the top floor terminating in a TPIC switch fuse near the control.
3 Provide proper and safe power supply for MRL on the lift top floor and for Machine Room type Elevator in the Machine Room during the installation and permanent operation of elevators. Electric power of 415V 50Hz 3 Phase terminating in suitable main switches for power and lighting circuits with necessary breakers accessories like MCB’s (rating as mentioned in our General arrangement drawing), suitable earthling leads and other protective device necessary to meet legal code requirements.
4 Suitable lightings of the shaft way and overhead exhaust fan, rubber mat, fire extinguisher and First Aid box to be provided. For Machine Room Type Lift, the Machine Room Shall be adequately lit with Fan and Exhaust.
5 The lift power wiring (Main Cable) should be taken from outside lift shaft. The single-phase wiring for lift bulk head fitting also to be taken from ground floor to lift the top floor separately for each lift.
DURING ERECTION &COMMISSIONING
1 Minor Builder Works such as Hole cutting and grouting of landing push button boxes, indicator. Grouting of control board, etc
2 Cleaning down, renewal or replacement of lift equipment damaged by dust to dry grinding of mosaic or other builders work.
3 Sill support & Header support channel/ bracket to provide if the wall thickness is not as required.
4 Facials & Architraves fixing and decoration of the landing entrance. (Work will stop during this time).
5 If specifications like telephone, piped music, BMS Contracts, are being supplied in the elevators, then we will terminate the wirings for the same in the respective lift top floor, all subsequent work/wiring and including supply and installation of telephones speakers etc. will have to be carried out by other contractors.
